    Questions, answered.

    What counts as a run?

    One agent execution, start to finish. Every step, retry, and tool call inside it is included — no per-span metering.

    Do unused runs roll over?

    No, but you can change tiers at any time, and Harness warns you before you approach a cap — never after.

    What happens if I hit my run limit?

    Agents keep running. We notify you at 80% and 100%, and overage runs bill at your plan's rate — or hard-stop them with a policy.

    Can I self-host Harness?

    Yes, on Enterprise. The same console runs in your cloud, with dedicated support and upgrade windows.

    Do you offer startup discounts?

    Yes — early-stage teams get the Team tier free for a year. Apply from the billing page with your company details.

    How are evals billed?

    Eval runs bill like normal runs. Golden datasets, scorers, and regression reports are free to store.
